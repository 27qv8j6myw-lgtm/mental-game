```
About the app
Mental Game is a golf scorecard app. Players in the same round enter hole-by-hole scores on their own phones, and the app calculates the settlement at the end of the round (the amounts players agreed on for their game, and splitting the caddie fee). The app does not accept, hold, or transfer money. It only calculates amounts; players settle with each other outside the app. There are no in-app purchases or ads.

How to sign in
Tap "Sign in with Apple" on the login screen. Any Apple ID works. Kakao and Google sign-in are also available.

How to try the main flow (one device is enough, about 2 minutes)
1. After signing in, enter any name.
2. Tap + at the top left of the home screen to create a round. Enter any golf course name, keep the default game, and tap the blue button at the bottom ("라운드 만들고 코드 받기").
3. In the waiting room, tap "＋ 폰 없는 동반자 추가" to add 2 players who have no phone, then tap "게임 시작" (Start game).
4. On each hole, tap a score for each player and tap 저장 (Save). The settlement updates as you go.
5. Tap the red 종료 (End) button and confirm. The results screen shows each player's amount.
6. On the home screen, tap "골프 룰" to see illustrated golf rules.

Account deletion
Profile tab (bottom right) → "회원 탈퇴" (Delete account) next to your name. For accounts that use Sign in with Apple, the app asks Apple to confirm once more and revokes the Sign in with Apple token.

Native features
Sign in with Apple (AuthenticationServices), saving result images to Photos, the iOS share sheet, native alerts, and an offline cache for golf courses with weak signal.
```
